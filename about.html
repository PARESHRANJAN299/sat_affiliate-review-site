About SAT Sports Gear

Last updated: October 2026

SAT Sports Gear is an independent sports and fitness equipment review site built for Indian buyers. We compare products across Amazon, Flipkart, and brand stores like Decathlon and Puma, and tell you plainly which ones are actually worth your money — and which aren't.

What we do

We research categories in depth — badminton rackets, running shoes, home gym equipment, and more — and put together honest buying guides based on specifications, pricing, and real user feedback. Where possible, we aim to test products ourselves rather than simply rewording a manufacturer's description.

How we make money

When you click a "Check Price" button on our site and buy something, we may earn a small commission from the retailer — at no extra cost to you. This is how we keep the site free and ad-light. Our full policy is on the Affiliate Disclosure page.

What we don't do

We don't sell products ourselves, process payments, or handle orders — every purchase happens directly on the retailer's own website (Amazon, Flipkart, or the relevant brand store). We're a research layer, not a marketplace.

Get in touch

Have a product you'd like us to review, or spotted something inaccurate in one of our guides? Contact us — we read every message.
