About SAT

Last updated: 15 October 2026

SAT is an independent buying-guide site for sports and fitness gear sold in India. We start with badminton and plan to add more.

What we do

We pick products sold in India and compare them by who they suit. We read the maker's details, the shop listings and what buyers say online. Right now our picks are based on research, not hands-on testing, and every guide says so. Read the steps on How We Pick.

How the site is funded

Right now it earns nothing and shows no ads. We plan to join affiliate programs, which pay a commission when you buy through a link, at no extra cost to you. We will say so clearly before that happens. See the Affiliate Disclosure.

What we don't do

We don't sell products, take orders or handle payments. You buy on the shop's own website. No brand owns SAT.

Who runs it

SAT is an independent project run from India.

Get in touch

Spotted a mistake, or want us to cover a product? Contact us.
