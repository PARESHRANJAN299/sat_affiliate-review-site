Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: 15 October 2026

SAT is an independent buying-guide site. We plan to join affiliate programs run by retailers such as Amazon India, Flipkart and Decathlon. Today we have not been approved by any of them.

What that means right now

The shop buttons on this site are plain links to each shop's search page. If you click one or buy something, we earn nothing. We show no ads.

What will change

When we start earning a commission, we will say so on this page first and add the date. We will also say it near the buttons. A commission is paid by the shop and does not cost you extra. The price you pay is the same.

Does money decide our picks?

No. We pick products by who they suit, using the steps on our How We Pick page. No brand or retailer has paid us to be included. If that ever changes, we will tell you here.

Where you buy

You always buy on the shop's own website, such as Amazon, Flipkart or Decathlon. We do not take orders or payments, and we do not sell any product.

Questions

Write to us through the Contact page.
