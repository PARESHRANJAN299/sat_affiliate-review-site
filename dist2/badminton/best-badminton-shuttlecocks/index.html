How to choose

Nylon or feather. Nylon lasts much longer and suits practice, beginners and casual play. Feather gives the natural flight and feel used in competition, but it breaks quickly. Sports Galaxy puts feathers at roughly 1 to 3 games and nylon at roughly 10 to 20 or more.

Check your event rules. Sports Galaxy says nylon is not approved for official tournaments. It says BWF-sanctioned events need feathers, while some local events may allow nylon. Ask the organiser before you buy.

Understand the speed number. Feather tubes usually carry a number from 76 to 79. A higher number means a heavier, faster shuttle that flies farther. A lower number means a lighter, slower one. For Yonex Mavis nylon, speed is set by cap colour: green is slow, blue is medium and red is fast.

Match speed to your court. Sports Galaxy suggests 76 for warm courts (about 25 to 30 C). It suggests 77 for moderate ones (about 20 to 25 C), and calls it the most common in India. It suggests 78 for cool or AC halls. These are shop habits, not an official BWF table, and sources disagree on the exact ranges.

Do a quick speed check. Sports Galaxy suggests a full underhand clear from the back line. The shuttle should land between the back doubles service line and the back boundary. If it falls short, try a faster one. If it goes long, try a slower one.

Check pack size, stock and the seller. Nylon packs here have 6 shuttles and feather tubes usually have 12. Some shops sell 2 tubes together. Stock changes often, so check current availability. Words like 'BWF approved' or 'Olympic shuttle' are seller text. Buy from authorised sellers and confirm with the maker or event rules.

Common questions

Should a beginner buy nylon or feather shuttlecocks?

Most shops and guides suggest nylon for beginners and practice, because it lasts longer. Feather suits players who want the natural flight and feel of competition, but it wears out quickly.

Can I use nylon shuttlecocks in tournaments?

Not in official ones. Sports Galaxy says BWF-sanctioned events need feathers, though some local recreational events may allow nylon. Check your event's rules.

Which speed should I choose for India?

Sports Galaxy calls 77 the most common choice in India. It suggests 76 for hot courts and 78 for cool or AC halls. This is a shop guide, not an official table. Try the underhand clear check on your own court.

How long does a feather shuttlecock last?

Not long. Sports Galaxy estimates about 1 to 3 games each. Hard smashes can wear feathers faster, and dry or AC air makes them break more easily. Shops advise keeping the tube sealed and away from sun and heat.
