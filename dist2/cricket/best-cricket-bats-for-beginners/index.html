How to choose

Match the bat to the ball. Tennis-ball bats are not built for leather balls. The Decathlon tennis-ball bat in this guide is listed as Kashmir poplar. Pick the bat for the ball you will actually play with.

Kashmir willow or English willow. Kashmir willow is the common lower-cost wood for leather-ball bats in India. English willow is generally lighter and has a bigger sweet spot, but it needs more care.

Understand grades. English willow bats come in grades. G1 is usually the top grade, with more straight grains and fewer marks. Decathlon's range runs from G5 to G1. Grades differ by brand, so compare grain count and weight on each listing.

Check size and weight. Choose size by player height. Shorter players usually need junior sizes like 5 or 6, and adults commonly use SH. Look for the weight in grams on the listing. None of the three Decathlon pages we opened gives a weight, so ask the seller or check in a store.

Plan for knocking-in and oiling. Shops and makers advise oiling and knocking-in an English willow bat before real use. Some shops offer this as a paid service. Do not skip it.

Read returns, warranty and the box. The three Decathlon India pages we opened show 30-day returns and a 2-year warranty. Small sellers often have shorter or conditional return windows. Also check for a cover and grip. Before you buy, make sure the page opens and shows stock.

Common questions

Can I use a tennis-ball bat with a leather ball?

No. Tennis-ball bats are not made for leather balls. Use a leather-ball bat for a leather ball.

Is Kashmir willow or English willow better for a beginner?

Kashmir willow is the usual lower-cost choice for a first leather-ball bat. English willow is generally lighter with a bigger sweet spot, but it needs oiling, knocking-in and more care. Start with Kashmir willow unless you will play often.

What size cricket bat should I buy?

Go by height. Adults commonly use SH. Shorter players usually need junior sizes like 5 or 6. Check the size chart on the product page, and try the bat in a store if you can.

Do I need to knock in a new bat?

Shops and makers advise oiling and knocking-in an English willow bat before real use. Some shops do it for a fee. The Decathlon pages we opened do not say if their bats come prepared, so ask before you buy.
