How to choose

Start with your skill level. The maker of the Domyos JR100 says it is a basic rope with no bearings, made for learning at lower speeds. If you are new to skipping, a basic rope like this is a simple place to start.

Know what a speed rope is. A speed rope has a bearing in the handle (a small part that lets the rope spin freely) and a thin cable. The Domyos speed rope has a 2.5 mm cable and weighs 130 g in all. The maker says it is made for fast skipping.

Check the surface. The Domyos pages for the basic, speed and weighted ropes all advise indoor use or smooth, non-abrasive floors. The speed rope page also says to avoid outdoor tarmac and rough surfaces. A mat or smooth indoor floor is the safe choice.

Check how the length adjusts. These ropes are about 3 m to 3.4 m long and adjustable. The Malta page says the handles should reach your armpits when sized. The speed rope needs a tool and screws to adjust, so keep that tool safe.

Think about weighted handles. Weighted ropes add resistance. On the Domyos 700 the weight is two removable 90 g pieces in the handles, so you can start without them.

Look at reviews and spare parts. Look at the number of reviews as well as the score. Decathlon India shows about 7,000 reviews for the basic rope, 749 for the speed rope and 233 for the weighted one. Also check whether a spare rope exists. Many pages list specs only on non-India Decathlon sites, so confirm length, weight, bearing type and box contents on the exact India listing or in store.

Common questions

Which skipping rope is best for a beginner?

The Domyos JR100 is the one the maker describes as made for learning to skip. It has no bearings and is meant for lower speeds. The speed rope's maker also says it suits beginners, but it is built for fast skipping, so the basic rope may be the simpler start.

Do I need a rope with bearings?

Not to start. The maker of the basic rope says it is for learning, and it has no bearings. A bearing lets the handle spin freely, which the maker of the speed rope offers for fast skipping and double unders.

Can I skip outdoors with these ropes?

The maker advises indoor use or smooth, non-abrasive floors for all three. For the speed rope, it says to avoid outdoor tarmac and rough surfaces. A TPU cover is sold separately in Malta, but we could not confirm it is sold in India.

Are these ropes available on Amazon.in or Flipkart?

We could not confirm that. All three are listed on Decathlon India, and that is the only shop we can point to. Stock can change by store and pincode, so check the listing before you plan to buy.
