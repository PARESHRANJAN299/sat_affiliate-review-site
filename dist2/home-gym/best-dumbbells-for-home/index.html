How to choose

Start light and go up in small steps. Pick a weight you can lift with control. Start light and move up slowly. This is general advice, not medical advice. If you are unsure, ask a qualified trainer or doctor.

Plan for more than one weight. Most people end up wanting at least two weights. A lighter one suits smaller muscles, and a heavier one suits larger muscles.

Fixed or adjustable. Fixed dumbbells need no setup, but you buy each weight separately. Plate-loaded kits cover a range in one box, but changing the weight takes longer. The maker of the Decathlon kit says its weights adjust in 1 kg steps.

Check one dumbbell or a pair. Read the title and the pack contents. Check if the weight shown is per dumbbell or the total. Some Decathlon India pages do not spell this out, so look closely before you order.

Look at the shape and coating. A hex head is made to stop a dumbbell rolling. Plate kits use bare cast iron, so keep them dry and wipe them with a damp cloth, as the maker says. Check the page for what the coating really is.

Read the exact product page. Listings can disagree. The 50 kg kit appears under two brands with different ratings, and a page address can say something different from the page text. Warranty and return terms also differ by country, so check the India page.

Common questions

How heavy should my first dumbbells be?

Pick a weight you can lift with control and move up slowly. Larger muscles usually handle more weight than smaller ones. This is general advice, not medical advice, so ask a qualified trainer if you are unsure.

Should I buy fixed or adjustable dumbbells?

Fixed ones are quick and need no setup, but you buy each weight separately. A plate kit gives a range in one box, but changing discs is slower.

Is the 10 kg Corength listing one dumbbell or two?

The title is singular, so it is likely one dumbbell and you would buy two for a pair. The page does not say this outright, so check before you order.

Do these come with a warranty?

The Decathlon India pages show a 2 year warranty badge and a 30 day replacement badge. The terms sit behind a link we did not read, so check the policy on the page.
