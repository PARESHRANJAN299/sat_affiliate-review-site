How to choose

Pick the shape first. Bands come in three common shapes. Short loops suit legs and glutes. Long loops help with pull-ups, stretching and strength work. Tube bands with handles suit rows, presses and curls. Choose the shape that matches what you plan to do.

Do not trust the kg number too much. The kg label is the maker's own rating, and makers do not all use the same scale. Compare levels inside one maker's range, not across brands. Check the figure on the pack, as listings can be unclear.

Check the material. The Domyos bands here are listed as latex or rubber, and the tube as elastodiene. If you have a latex allergy, look for a listing that clearly says latex-free.

Look at width and thickness. Wider and thicker bands are generally stronger. Compare size details only within one maker's range.

Set or single band. A set with several levels lets you move up as you get stronger. A single band is a simpler start, but you may need to buy more later.

Stay safe and look after the band. For pull-up help, anchor the band to a bar you trust and check the hooks are fully engaged. Look for nicks or cracks before each use. Keep bands away from sunlight, heat and sharp things.

Read warranty and returns. These listings show a 2-year warranty badge and a returns link. We did not read the full terms, so check them on the shop page before you buy.

Common questions

Which resistance band is good for beginners?

A multi-level set is a gentle way to start. The Domyos mini loop set gives three levels. The Domyos tube with handles suits you if you want rows and presses. Pick by what you want to train.

Can I use these bands if I have a latex allergy?

Not the Domyos bands in this guide. They are listed as latex or rubber, and the tube as elastodiene. The Corength band's material is not listed. Look for a clearly latex-free listing instead.

Do I need a pull-up bar for the Corength band?

Yes. The maker says it works with wall bars, door bars and weight-training racks, but the bar is not included. Make sure the bar is firm and the hooks are fully engaged before you use it.
