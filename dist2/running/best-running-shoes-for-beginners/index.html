Our three picks

For first-timers

Kalenji Run Active (men's, Comfort Fit)

Verdict: A simple first pair for light, occasional jogging. The shop page shows a 2-year warranty and a 30-day exchange.

This is Decathlon's own Kalenji brand. The Decathlon India page lists it as a men's Comfort Fit running shoe for up to 10 km a week. The maker positions it for light running, which fits a new runner who is not sure how much they will run.

Know the limits before you buy. The page does not give the weight, heel-to-toe drop (how much higher the heel sits than the toe) or upper material. Sizes are listed, but the page does not say which size system is used. The title and heading also disagree on colour, so check the colour at checkout. We could not confirm how it fits, how long it lasts, or whether a women's version is sold in India.

Good

  • The maker positions it for up to 10 km a week, which suits light jogging as a first pair.
  • The Decathlon India page shows a 2-year warranty and a 30-day exchange, which gives room to fix a wrong size.
  • The shop page shows about 4.6 out of 5 from several thousand ratings, so it is not a handful of opinions. No review text was visible.
  • It is sold through Decathlon's own site and stores, so you may be able to try it on in person (store stock not checked).

Not so good

  • It is meant for up to 10 km a week only, so you may outgrow it as your running grows.
  • The listing gives no weight, drop or upper material, so cushioning and durability cannot be checked.
  • Colour is shown inconsistently on the listing, so check it before you order.

For comfort-first beginners

ASICS Gel-Excite 10 (men's)

Verdict: A comfortable everyday shoe that one reviewer says suits new runners. Check stock first, because a newer version may have replaced it.

The Gel-Excite 10 is a neutral road running shoe. Neutral means it has no extra support built in for feet that roll inward. Retailers and review sites describe rearfoot GEL cushioning, a foam midsole and a mesh upper. Running Shoes Guru calls it a comfortable, breathable daily trainer that suits beginners.

Be careful with stock. In the shop pages we saw, the ASICS India colourway showed all sizes sold out, and the newer Gel-Excite 11 may be the version actually on sale. Weight and drop differ between sources (260 g to about 284 g, and 8 mm or 10 mm), so we treat them as unconfirmed. We could not load the asics.co.in pages, and we did not find an aggregate rating from an Indian shop page. Wide-width availability in India is also not confirmed.

Good

  • Running Shoes Guru calls it a comfortable, smooth, neutral daily trainer that suits beginners and runs up to half-marathon distance.
  • The same reviewer found it true to size, with a roomier-than-it-looks upper and room for shoe inserts.
  • Reviewers and retailers describe balanced, medium cushioning with GEL at the heel.
  • At least one Nykaa Man listing names ASICS India as the seller, which is worth checking when you buy.

Not so good

  • Running Shoes Guru rates durability as moderate (7.0 out of 10). Owners there report anything from an upper tearing in about 5 months to about 400 miles of use.
  • That reviewer lists low support as the main con and does not recommend it for heavy weekly mileage such as full-marathon training.
  • Sellers and colourways differ between listings, and stock looks doubtful, so check the seller and the version before you buy.

For regular runners

Nike Pegasus 42 (men's road running)

Verdict: A daily trainer that one reviewer rates as very durable. Reviewers disagree on how soft and how roomy it feels.

The Pegasus 42 is a daily road trainer. The nike.in listing mentions a ReactX foam midsole and an Air Zoom unit, and says the updated fit gives more room in the forefoot and toe box. Nike lists a Wide version too. It is the premium tier of these three picks, so it may be more shoe than you need if you are unsure you will keep running.

The India listing does not give the weight, drop or outsole details. Reviewers report a drop of about 10 mm and a weight of roughly 283 to 292 g. The nike.in page shows no customer rating and no warranty period. We did not check Amazon.in, Flipkart or other shops, and we do not know if the Wide version is stocked in every size in India.

Good

  • Running Shoes Guru rates durability 9 out of 10 and praises the tough outsole and the soft, padded upper.
  • Reviewers say the 10 mm drop and heel cushioning suit people who land on their heels.
  • RTINGS found the length true to size, with a lot of room through the arch to the ball of the foot.
  • nike.in lists a Wide version and offers return or replacement within 14 days of delivery.

Not so good

  • Reviewers disagree on fit. Running Shoes Guru says it runs small with a tight toe box and suggests half a size up. RTINGS calls it accommodating, though the forefoot tapers.
  • RTINGS rates cushioning only 6 out of 10 and Running Shoes Guru calls the midsole flat and dated. Reviewers also say it is heavier than many daily trainers.
  • Returns need the product in original condition with tags, started from the app within 14 days.

How to choose

Fit comes first. Try shoes on with running socks, and leave about a thumb's width of space in front of your longest toe.

Check the toe box and width. Look for a roomy toe box or a Wide version, such as the one Nike lists for the Pegasus 42. Check each brand's size chart, because sizing differs.

Do not trust one review on sizing. Reviewers can disagree even on one shoe. The Pegasus 42 toe box is called tight by one reviewer and accommodating by another. Treat sizing advice as a hint and try the shoe on if you can.

Check if it is neutral or a stability shoe. Running Shoes Guru describes the ASICS Gel-Excite 10 as neutral with medium cushioning. Shops usually say whether a shoe is neutral or a stability shoe, so check before you buy.

Read the maker's stated use. Decathlon says the Run Active is for up to 10 km a week. A stated weekly limit tells you when you may outgrow the shoe.

Check returns and the seller. Return terms differ: nike.in shows 14 days with tags attached, Decathlon India shows a 30-day exchange and a 2-year warranty, and some resellers allow only 7 days. Buy from the maker's store or an authorised seller, and check the seller name, since sellers, colourways and stock can differ for the same shoe.

Common questions

Which of these is the easiest first pair?

The Kalenji Run Active is made for light running of up to 10 km a week, and Decathlon India shows a 30-day exchange. One reviewer also calls the ASICS Gel-Excite 10 good for beginners, but check that it is in stock.

What does heel-to-toe drop mean?

It is how much higher the heel sits than the toe. Reviewers say a higher drop, around 8 to 10 mm, tends to suit people who land on their heels.

How do I know when to replace my shoes?

Look at the sole and the cushioning, and replace the pair when they look worn. Owner reports of durability vary a lot, even for the same shoe, so treat review scores as a guide only.

Should I buy online or in a store?

A store is better for fit, because you can try the shoe on. If you buy online, check the return terms and the seller name first.
