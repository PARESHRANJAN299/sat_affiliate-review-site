How to choose

Pick the thickness for your floor and poses. Thick mats of 8 to 10 mm give more padding for knees on hard floors. They feel soft and less steady in standing and balance poses. About 4 to 6 mm is the usual middle ground.

Know your material. NBR is a soft, light, thick foam sold for cushioning. Buying guides say it suits fast, standing-heavy or hot yoga less well. TPE is often described as light and soft. Natural rubber is often described as denser, with a tackier grip. These are general descriptions, not test results.

Read the material on the exact listing. Some Indian listings contradict themselves. A mat may be named one material while the details say another. Read the full product details before you buy.

Look at the grip layer. Grip depends on the top layer as well as the base. A PU top can be grippy, but Decathlon warns that dust, creams and sweat wear it down. Wipe it regularly. On NBR or closed-cell mats, a towel can help if your hands get sweaty.

Check the size if you are tall. Manduka's PRO comes in 68, 71 and 85 inch lengths. Decathlon India's yoga mat category lists some longer mats too. We could not confirm a longer version of the Kimjaly 4 mm.

Check who sells it and the return terms. Mats sold by the maker or a large shop often have clearer warranty and return terms, like the 2-year warranty and 30-day returns on decathlon.in. Third-party or cross-border sellers can differ. Confirm the terms before you rely on a brand guarantee.

Common questions

What thickness of yoga mat should I choose?

It depends on your floor and your poses. Thick mats of 8 to 10 mm are comfier for knees but less steady for standing poses. About 4 to 6 mm is a common middle choice.

Is an NBR mat good for hot yoga?

Buying guides say NBR is not the best fit for fast, standing-heavy or hot yoga. If your hands get sweaty, you may want a towel. NBR suits gentle stretching and floor work better.

How do I keep a PU-top mat grippy?

Wipe it regularly. Decathlon warns that dust, creams and sweat can wear down the PU grip layer over time. For the Kimjaly, an overseas Decathlon listing suggests warm soapy water and a microfibre cloth, then air drying away from the sun.

Will a brand's guarantee work if I buy from a third-party seller?

Not always. Manduka advertises a lifetime guarantee, but we could not confirm that it covers mats sold into India through third-party or cross-border sellers. Check the terms on the exact listing before you buy.
