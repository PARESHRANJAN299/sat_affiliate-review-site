{{site_name}} is an independent sports and fitness equipment review site built for Indian buyers. We compare products across Amazon, Flipkart, and brand stores like Decathlon and Puma, and tell you plainly which ones are actually worth your money — and which aren't.

What we do

We research categories in depth — badminton rackets, running shoes, home gym equipment, and more — and put together honest buying guides based on specifications, pricing, and real user feedback. Right now our picks are research-based, and we say so on each page. We plan to add hands-on testing and will mark clearly which products we have tested ourselves.

How we make money

When you click a "Check Price" button on our site and buy something, we may earn a small commission from the retailer — at no extra cost to you. This is how we keep the site free and ad-light. Our full policy is on the Affiliate Disclosure page.

What we don't do

We don't sell products ourselves, process payments, or handle orders — every purchase happens directly on the retailer's own website (Amazon, Flipkart, or the relevant brand store). We're a research layer, not a marketplace.
