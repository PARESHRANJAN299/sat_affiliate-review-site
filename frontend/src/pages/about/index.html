What we do

We research products and organise what we find, so you can compare options more easily. For each product or product type, we aim to explain:

  • what it is useful for
  • its pros and cons
  • typical price ranges in India
  • its main features
  • who it suits, and who it does not
  • alternatives worth considering
  • whether it gives good value for money

What we do not do

{{site_name}} does not manufacture or directly sell products, and it does not take orders or payments. If you decide to buy, you buy from a retailer or a brand's own website.

How we work

Our recommendations come from research, not from personal testing unless a page clearly says otherwise. Read How We Pick to see what we look at.
