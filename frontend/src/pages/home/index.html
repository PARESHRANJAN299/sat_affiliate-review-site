How we work

Three steps, nothing hidden.

  1. We research

    We read the maker's details, the shop listings and what buyers say. We have not hand-tested any product yet, and every guide says so. How we pick.

  2. We compare

    We match each product to who it suits, such as beginners, regular players or strong players. No scores, no made-up ratings.

  3. You buy from the shop

    Our buttons open the shop's own page. We take no orders or payments. Right now our links earn us nothing and we show no ads. See our disclosure.
