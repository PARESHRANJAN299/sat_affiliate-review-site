How We Pick Products

Last updated: {{updated_human}}

This is how every guide on {{site_name}} is made, and what it can and cannot tell you.

What we do

  1. Choose a product type that Indian shoppers search for, such as badminton rackets.
  2. Find products sold in India on shops like Amazon, Flipkart and Decathlon.
  3. Read the maker's details, the shop listings and what buyers say online.
  4. Compare them by who they suit, such as beginners, regular players or strong players.
  5. Write a short verdict for each, with what is good and what is not.

What we look at

Weight, balance, build, what comes in the box, warranty and returns, common buyer complaints, and the risk of fake copies. We do not show prices, because they change every day. The shop shows the real price.

What we do not do yet

We do not test products ourselves yet. Every guide says that we have not tested the products ourselves. If we start testing, we will mark exactly which products we tested.

Limits

Details can differ from seller to seller. When we cannot confirm something, we say so instead of guessing. Always check the listing before you pay.

Independence

No brand or retailer has paid us to be included. Read more on the Affiliate Disclosure page.

Corrections

If you find a mistake, tell us. We fix it and update the date on the guide.
