How We Pick

Last updated: {{updated_human}}

This page explains how {{site_name}} decides which products to recommend, and what our recommendations are and are not.

What we consider

Depending on the product, a recommendation may consider:

  • how useful the product is for its intended use
  • its specifications and features
  • build and material quality
  • customer feedback, where it is available
  • value for money
  • the typical price range in India
  • brand reputation
  • competing alternatives

Research, not testing

Our recommendations are based on research: maker and retailer information, specifications and published customer feedback. We do not claim to have personally tested a product unless a page clearly says so.

If we start hands-on testing in the future, we will mark clearly which products were tested, and keep those separate from products we only researched.

What you will not find here

We do not publish made-up ratings, made-up reviews or made-up testing claims. If we could not confirm something, we say so.

Prices

Prices change often. We show typical price ranges, not live prices. Check the retailer for the latest price.

Corrections

If something is wrong or out of date, please tell us. We will check it and update the page.
