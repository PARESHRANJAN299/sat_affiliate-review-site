This page explains, in plain words, what happens to your information when you visit {{site_name}}.

What we collect

We do not collect anything ourselves. This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. It loads no fonts or code from other companies.

What our host collects

The site is delivered by Cloudflare. Like most hosts, Cloudflare keeps standard technical logs, such as your IP address, browser type and the page you asked for, to run and protect the site. We do not use these logs to identify you.

If you email us

We use your email address and message only to reply to you. We do not sell them or share them with anyone.

Links to other sites

Our pages link to shops such as Amazon, Flipkart and Decathlon. Once you click through, their cookies and privacy policies apply, not ours. Please read them before you buy.

If this changes

If we add analytics or affiliate tracking later, we will update this page before it goes live, and change the date above.
