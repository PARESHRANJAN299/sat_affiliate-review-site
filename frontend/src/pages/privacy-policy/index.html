Privacy Policy

This page explains, in simple words, what information {{site_name}} may collect and how it is used.

Information you send us

If you contact us, we receive what you choose to send: your name, your email address and your message. The contact form on this site opens your own email app, so your message reaches us as an ordinary email. We use it only to reply to you and to improve the site. We do not sell your personal information.

Analytics and cookies

{{site_name}} does not currently run analytics or advertising tools, and does not set cookies of its own. The site may keep small settings in your browser's storage, for example to remember that you have already seen a hint or an animation. These stay on your device and are not sent to us. If we turn on analytics later, which may collect basic data such as pages visited, we will update this page first. Our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs, for example IP address, browser and device information, to run and protect the site.

Links to other websites

Our pages link to retailers, brands and other third-party websites. Those sites have their own privacy policies and cookies, which apply after you click through. {{site_name}} is not responsible for their privacy practices, so please read their policies.

Amazon and other affiliate links

{{site_name}} takes part in the Amazon Associates Programme. Product links to Amazon.in carry our Associates tracking ID, so Amazon can tell that a visit came from {{site_name}}. When you click one of these links you leave our site. Amazon may then set cookies or use similar tools on your device and collect information under its own privacy notice, which you can find at the bottom of every Amazon.in page. We do not receive your name or contact details from Amazon. We only see Amazon's reports about clicks and qualifying purchases.

Links to other retailers may also carry tracking information in the same way. Every affiliate link is marked, and our Affiliate Disclosure explains how we earn from them.

Your requests

You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to {{contact_email}}.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change, and change the date at the top.
