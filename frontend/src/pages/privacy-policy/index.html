Privacy Policy

This page explains, in simple words, what information {{site_name}} may collect and how it is used.

Information you send us

If you contact us, we receive what you choose to send: your name, your email address and your message. The contact form on this site opens your own email app, so your message reaches us as an ordinary email. We use it only to reply to you and to improve the site. We do not sell your personal information.

Analytics and browser storage

SAT uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand visits, page views, referral sources, device and browser types, countries and page-loading performance. Its browser script sends analytics and performance measurements to Cloudflare. We use aggregate reports to improve the website; we do not use this setup to ask visitors for names, phone numbers or email addresses.

Cloudflare describes Web Analytics as privacy-first and does not use it to track individuals across its customers’ websites. Read Cloudflare’s data-collection explanation and Privacy Policy for details.

The site may keep small settings in your browser’s storage, for example to remember a hint or animation. These settings stay on your device. Cloudflare may also process technical information, including IP addresses and request logs, to deliver and protect the site. Third-party retailers may use cookies after you follow their links, as explained below.

Links to other websites

Our pages link to retailers, brands and other third-party websites. Those sites have their own privacy policies and cookies, which apply after you click through. {{site_name}} is not responsible for their privacy practices, so please read their policies.
